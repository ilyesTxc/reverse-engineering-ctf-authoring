The Mad Smith's Forge - The Deeper Trial

Hear ye, brave seeker! Thou hast pried open the Great Gate once,
and the Mad Smith is not amused. He has dug deeper beneath the ruined keep,
forged a mightier key in more pieces than any one hand can count,
and strewn the forge with counterfeits to mock thy craft.
The Forge and the Great Gate stand once more amid the rubble,
and both now keep watch upon those who watch them.
Prove thy worth a second time, claim the deeper treasure,
and bring back the secret of Chaos.
